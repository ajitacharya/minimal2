Privacy Policy

At Minimal, we are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit or make a purchase from our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: such as your name, email address, billing address, and phone number.
  • Payment Information: processed securely through third-party providers (we never store full card details).
  • Usage Data: including your IP address, browser type, and device information to help improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us provide a better experience. We may use it to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders.
  • Communicate order updates, offers, or customer support messages.
  • Improve website performance, design, and user experience.
  • Comply with legal or tax obligations.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. While we strive for complete security, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can adjust cookie preferences through your browser settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your data. However, we may share limited information with trusted third parties such as:

  • Payment processors (for secure transactions).
  • Shipping providers (to deliver your orders).
  • Analytics platforms (to understand user behavior).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and request a copy of your data.
  • Request corrections or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for business, legal, or tax purposes. When no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize your information.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. Minimal is not responsible for their privacy practices or content. Please review their policies before providing any personal data.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we learn that a child’s data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date below.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@minimal.com.
